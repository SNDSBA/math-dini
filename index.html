Interactive Palm Oil Clarification Rate Modeler

The Bioscience Scenario

During crude palm oil (CPO) extraction in Malaysian mills, clarifying tanks separate pure edible oil from water and heavy organic solids. This app models a standard 1,000-liter batch settling unit operated at 90°C.

By tracking the derivative $V'(t)$, food scientists find the precise peak efficiency window, ensuring minimum oil loss and lower processing footprints.

Data Baseline Reference: Malaysian Palm Oil Board (mpob.gov.mop) Technical Guidelines & ScienceDirect / Journal of Food Engineering.

Mathematical Modeling

Yield Function $V(t)$: V(t) = L / (1 + e^(-k * (t - t0)))
Separation Rate $V'(t)$ (First Derivative): V'(t) = k * V(t) * (1 - V(t)/L)
Where max capacity $L = 1000\text{ L}$, growth constant $k = 0.85$, and inflection midpoint $t_0 = 4.0\text{ hours}$.
